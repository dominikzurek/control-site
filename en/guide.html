First steps

Three minutes are enough to start.

The first start
You pick the language and the modules you want, then add your first account on the Money screen (e.g. “Bank” or “Cash”) with its balance. You can change everything later.
The bottom bar
Money (the start screen), Today, Calendar, Progress and More. More holds the modules, the tools and Settings.
Modules
More → Modules. A module you switch off disappears from the screens; its data stays on the phone for later. Start with one or two and add more once they become a habit.

Money

The main module, free for good. It works best when you add expenses right away: it takes a few seconds.

A new expense or income
The “Add” button on the Money screen: amount, category, account. Tap an entry in the list to change or delete it. A #word in the note (e.g. #holidays) gathers the costs of one trip or renovation.
You can spend today
The amount for today until the next pay day (set the payday in the Money settings; without it, until the end of the month), after the payments, installments and debts due before it. Look in the morning, before you spend anything.
Recurring payments
Money → settings → Recurring payments. Rent, subscriptions, salary: save them once and CONTROL reminds you of the date and offers to book them. They also feed “You can spend today”, the forecast and the next 7 days.
Accounts and categories
The settings icon at the top of the Money screen. Recurring payments, the payday, exchange rates, bank notifications, the evening reminder and the bank import are here too.
Money tools
At the bottom of the Money screen: a budget (a monthly limit that warns at 80%), a 60-day forecast, savings jars, “I want to buy” (wait before buying), challenges (a pause in spending or 52 weeks of saving), fixed costs a month and a year, a loan calculator, debts (what you owe and what others owe you), splitting a bill, category rules, tags, import from the bank (CSV), export for Excel and a PDF report of a month or a year.
Loan calculator
The installment, the interest and what an overpayment changes. “Installment schedule” shows every month: how much of the installment is principal, how much is interest and what is left to pay.
Bank notifications
Optional: Money → settings → Bank notifications. After your consent and choosing your bank app, CONTROL turns a payment notification into a suggested entry under “To check”. You confirm or correct it. CONTROL does not connect to your bank and does not see your account.
Receipt scan
In a new expense, the receipt icon: take a photo or pick one from the gallery, and the total, the shop and the date fill in. The phone reads the text; the photo is not kept.
Evening reminder
Money → settings → “Evening reminder to note spending”. The notification has buttons for your three most used categories: a tap opens a new expense already in that category.
Analysis
The icon at the top of the Money screen: where the money goes over months and years, compared with the earlier ones.

Today, Calendar and Progress

The daily overview in one place.

Today
The day plan, habits to tick off, payments to book and the next 7 days: pay day, bills, installments, birthdays, the car’s inspection and insurance. A fast in progress shows here too.
Calendar
Every day with what happened in it. Purple bars mark days with money: pay day or planned payments.
Progress
How it goes over time: health, habits and impulse control (money: Analysis on the Money screen). No points and no streaks that reset: what counts is the share of good days.

Habits

A new habit sticks when it is small and tied to something you already do.

A new habit
More → Habits. Best as “after coffee: 5 minutes of reading”. You set the order and tick habits off on Today. Some habits (e.g. steps) can tick themselves off from your watch data.
The “Habits” widget
Settings → Widget “Habits”, or hold an empty spot on the home screen → Widgets → CONTROL. Tick a habit off with one tap without opening the app; CONTROL takes it in the next time it opens.

Health

Data from your watch and your own measures. CONTROL is not a medical device.

Your watch
More → Health settings. Through Health Connect you link Garmin, Samsung, Fitbit and more and choose which app the data comes from. CONTROL only reads: sleep, steps, exercise, heart rate, heart rate variability and active calories burned.
Calories
You type in what you eat (a meal); the calories burned come from your watch. Together they give the balance of the day.
Fasting
On the Health screen: start, a goal in hours and a reminder when the goal is reached. A fast in progress also shows on Today.

Impulse control

For habits you want to stop, e.g. mindless scrolling or buying on a whim.

How it works
You name the habit in your own words. When the urge comes, tap “I have an urge” on Today: a short protocol helps you wait, breathe and rate the urge again. An urge rises and falls even when you do not act on it.
A slip
It does not reset your progress. What counts is the share of good days in the last 7, 30, 90 and 365 days, not a single counter.
Help
More → Help: the emergency number and help lines in your country (where the app has them).

The BLOCKER

When willpower is not enough, change your surroundings: the apps you chose stay closed until the block ends.

A block now
More → Blocker. Choose the apps (and browsers) and the time. You cannot end a block early, on purpose. Phone, messages and Settings always work. The first time, Android asks for two permissions: usage access and display over other apps.
Block plan
In the Blocker: “Plan a block”, e.g. every evening 22:00–07:00 or on weekdays at work. The block starts by itself, even when CONTROL is closed.

Key dates, journal, Your year, breathing

Small tools under More.

Key dates
Birthdays and anniversaries every year, and the days things end: an ID card, a passport, a driving licence, a warranty, insurance, a free trial. The reminder comes ahead of time.
Evening journal
Three short questions at the end of the day: what went well, what you are grateful for and one thing for tomorrow. You can turn on an evening reminder.
Your year
The numbers of the year as a picture to share.
Breathe
Guided breathing from 1 to 10 minutes, for stress and before sleep.

CONTROL Pro

One yearly subscription in Google Play, the first 30 days free. Two days before the trial ends, CONTROL reminds you that it is ending.

What it adds
A planner with alarms and the phone calendar, Work (shift system, hours, expected pay), Goals with a plan for obstacles (WOOP), Focus (work blocks with breaks), Vehicles (inspection, insurance, service, fuel use, tyres), Bills (due dates, the history of prices, electricity, water and gas meters compared with a year earlier) and Shopping (lists, the cart in the shop, paid in Money).
Cancelling
In Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. After you cancel, Pro works until the end of the paid period, and your data stays.

Settings, backup and privacy

More → Settings.

Appearance
Dark, light or like the phone.
Language and currency
17 languages. Each account can have its own currency.
App lock
A PIN and your fingerprint or face. The fingerprint never reaches the app; Android checks it.
Backup
Settings → Backup: a JSON file by hand, or every week into a folder you choose (e.g. one synced with your cloud drive). On a new phone: make a backup, move the file and choose “Import JSON”.
Privacy
CONTROL has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything stays on your phone. To delete everything: Backup → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.

When something does not work

Reminders do not come
In Android settings allow CONTROL notifications and alarms & reminders. On some phones (e.g. Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung) also turn off battery saving for CONTROL.
A widget shows old data
Open CONTROL once: widgets refresh with every change in the app.
No data from the watch
Check that the watch app writes to Health Connect and that CONTROL has access (More → Health settings).
