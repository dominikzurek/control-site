What CONTROL can do on your phone

Every permission of an Android app is public. This is the full list of the release version, with what each one is for.

  • INTERNETNot there. CONTROL has no way to send anything: not your data, not statistics, not ads.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONSReminders for the check-in, habits, payments and the day plan, ringing alarms.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMAlarms and reminders that ring on time.
  • com.android.alarm.permission.SET_ALARM"Set an alarm in Clock": opens the phone's Clock with the time filled in.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDPuts reminders and alarms back after a restart.
  • USE_BIOMETRICLocking the app with your fingerprint, if you turn it on.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SYSTEM_EXEMPTEDThe BLOCKER, while you run it: a local VPN on the phone that cuts off the apps you chose. It reads nothing and sends nothing.
  • READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDARThe phone's calendar in the planner, only if you connect it.
  • health.READ_SLEEP, health.READ_STEPS, health.READ_EXERCISE, health.READ_HEART_RATE, health.READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE, health.READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITYSleep, steps, workouts and heart rate from your watch through Health Connect, if you connect it. Read only.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATSScreen time, if you allow it.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEThe subscription through the Play Store app on the phone (Google's billing library). Neither lets CONTROL send data.
