What CONTROL can do on your phone

Every permission of an Android app is public. This is the full list, with what each one is for. You can check it in your phone's settings or in the Exodus Privacy report.

  • android.permission.INTERNETNot there. CONTROL has no way to send anything: not your data, not statistics, not ads.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONSReminders for the check-in, habits and the day plan.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, SET_ALARMAlarms and reminders that ring on time.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDPuts reminders and alarms back after a restart.
  • USE_BIOMETRICLocking the app with your fingerprint.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICEBLOCKER: a local VPN on the phone that cuts off chosen sites. It sends nothing.
  • READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDARThe phone's calendar in the planner, only if you connect it.
  • health.READ_SLEEP, READ_STEPS, READ_EXERCISE, READ_HEART_RATE, READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE, READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITYSleep, steps, workouts and heart rate from your watch through Health Connect. Read only.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATSScreen time, if you allow it.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEThe subscription through the Play Store app on the phone (Google's billing library). Neither lets CONTROL send data.
